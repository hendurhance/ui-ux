# About the mirrors

## The short version

**Buy the books you can afford.** Every book in this guide links to its publisher first. The authors wrote these because they wanted people to learn from them; paying for that is how they keep writing.

The mirrors exist for people who genuinely cannot pay — students, career changers, and anyone in a country where a $50 design book costs a meaningful slice of a month's income. That is the only reason they are here.

## Where these files come from

The files mirrored here were collected from public shadow-library sources, principally Anna's Archive, Library Genesis, Z-Library, and OceanofPDF. Many filenames still carry those origins.

Mirror links point to MEGA. No claim is made that this constitutes licensed distribution, and nothing here should be read as permission to redistribute the works themselves.

## For authors, publishers, and rights holders

If your work is listed here and you want it gone, it will be removed. You do not need a lawyer, a formal DMCA notice, or any particular wording.

**[Open a takedown request](https://github.com/hendurhance/ui-ux/issues/new?template=03-takedown.yml)** — it takes a minute and needs no account details beyond your own.

If you would rather not post publicly, write to `hendurhance.dev [at] gmail [dot] com` instead. It reaches the same person.

Tell us which title, and whether you want:

- **the mirror removed** — the entry stays, pointing only at your purchase page, or
- **the whole entry removed** — the book disappears from the guide entirely

Most authors choose the first. Requests are handled ahead of everything else in this repository, and no justification is required beyond identifying yourself as the rights holder.

## What this document is not

This is not legal advice, and the maintainer is not a lawyer. It is a plain statement of what this repository does and how to get something removed from it.

The guide's text, structure, and descriptions are released under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). That license covers the writing in this repository only. It does not extend to the linked works, which remain the property of their authors and publishers.

## If you can pay, pay

Several books here are free because their authors chose to give them away — those carry a green **FREE** badge and link straight to the source. The rest cost money because someone spent a year or more writing them.

Buying one book you actually read is worth more than downloading twenty you never open.
